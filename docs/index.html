Android / Google TV

TV

The same APK installs on Android TV and Google TV (0.1.12 and newer). It opens TV mode, works with the remote, and you sign in with your phone by scanning a QR code.

  1. Install Downloader (by AFTVnews) from the Play Store on your TV, or send the APK with Send Files to TV.
  2. Open the latest release and install the .apk. Allow installs from that app when asked.
  3. SVCZZ then appears in your TV's apps row.
